# Shang Tsung defeated: the souls escape

MK1's ending, rebuilt on MK2's Shang. In the **one-player game** the CPU Shang
is now fought in **KAHN'S ARENA**, the first of the three that close the tower
(Shang, Kintaro, Shao Kahn), and when he loses the deciding round there is **no
FINISH HIM**. Instead the souls of every fighter he ever took tear their way out
of him, one at a time, and burn.

Built from `doc/MK1_SHANG_SOUL_ESCAPE.md` - the original, extracted from
`mk.zip` by `tools/mk1_shang_souls_extract.py`. Every tick count below is MK1's
own number.

```
tools\run_shang_soul_escape_test.ps1                  # the real path, automatic
tools\run_shang_soul_escape_test.ps1 -Mode control    # a non-Shang CPU still gets FINISH HIM
```

Code: `src/MKSOULS.ASM`, behind `soulesc .set 1` (MAINEQU.ASM). Set it to 0 and
Shang goes back to a normal FINISH HIM and the ordinary stage rotation.

## What plays

`t` is in game ticks (54.7068 Hz). Measured end to end at **702 ticks, ~12.8 s**.

| t | what | where it comes from |
|---|------|---------------------|
| 0 | The deciding KO. Six screen flashes, 4 ticks on / 4 off | the block fill (`srtflash`), MK1 `FFDD2990` |
| ~20 | He turns back into **himself** inside retail's Shang↔Kintaro **poof** (flash + flame), whatever form he lost in | `baby_flash` + `baby_poof` |
| ~40 | He **stumbles**, over and over: his whole `STSTUMBLE1-6` (TSUNG6.IMG) on a loop, 4 ticks a frame and ±7 px every step as MK1 shakes its Shang, while the arena's **sky flashes white** every 4 ticks and the background Kahn stands up off his throne | `a_soul_shake`, MK1 `FFDD30B0`/`FFDD3030`; `BGND.ASM` |
| ~120 on | **A soul every 27 ticks**, alternating sides, through all sixteen fighters | MK1 `FFDD31F0` |
| per soul | A stance copy of Shang in his colours, drifting clear at 2-5 px/tick, 8 ticks, with that fighter's scream | `FFDD39B0`, `FFDD3560` |
| | Shang's own **morph** into that fighter, 3 blend frames × 6 ticks | `st_anitab2`, via `FFD75550` |
| | It IS him now: **stumble back** (4 frames × 4), **knocked flat** (@3) | his `023h` and `00Fh` |
| | The **MK1 flame** bursts over the body and burns down, 5 ticks a frame; the soul is ash under it | `a_mk1fire` (Scorpion's scortch column) |
| ~650 | Sky back, six more flashes | |
| ~690 | Shang goes down on his own knockdown lane and stays there; the winner is declared the ordinary way | |

There is **no new art in any of this**, and none in MK1 either: a soul is a
Shang copy running his ordinary morph and the victim's ordinary reactions, and
the fire is the flame Scorpion's MK1 scortch already carries.

## The roster

All sixteen, in select-grid order then the secrets: Liu Kang, Kung Lao, Johnny
Cage, Reptile, Sub-Zero, Kitana, Jax, Mileena, Baraka, Scorpion, Raiden, Smoke,
Noob Saibot, Jade, Ermac, Khameleon. Not Shang (he is the one losing them), and
not Kintaro or Kahn.

Retail only authored Shang-into-X blend frames for the eleven he can morph into.
The other five are drawn from art that has them - the ninjas are Sub-Zero's and
Reptile's frames, Jade and Khameleon are Kitana's - so their souls borrow those
frames with a **palette of their own**, generated by
`tools/make_soul_blend_pals.py` into `src/SOULPAL.ASM`: the base fighter's blend
palette with its costume colours re-hued onto the target's, leaving Shang's skin
and hair where they were (19 of 61 colours for Smoke, 35 of 64 for Ermac, and so
on). Phase D regenerates and compares it, so a repaint of any source palette
fails the build instead of quietly blending through the old colours.

The soul that matches the winner's own fighter wears the **mirror-match**
palette (`character_palettes_2`), which is what MK1 does with it too.

## Shang's own colours - the trap

The CPU Shang almost always loses wearing somebody else, and losing puts his
`ochar` back to `ft_st` **without** putting his colours back - measured, his
object held `SUB_P` with `ochar = 6`, i.e. Shang's art in Sub-Zero's palette
(a pale blue figure with pink legs), and every soul copy inherited it.

`player_normpal` alone does not fix that: it only restores when `pm_alt_pal`
is up (`pnorm_check_temp_alt` - "not using an alternate palette, dont switch"),
and `shang_morph` applied the Sub-Zero colours THROUGH `player_normpal`, which
clears the flag. So the sequence raises `pm_alt_pal` first, exactly as
`player_swpal` would have, then calls it. The soul copies read his live
palette id back out of `palram[his colour map]` (`soul_live_pal`) rather than
assuming `character_palettes_1`, so they match him whatever he wears.

Shakes tried before the full stumble: `a_ststunned` (the FINISH HIM dizzy,
which reads as being hit), `a_drained` (his soul-drain wither) and
`STSTUMBLE1,2,3,2` (MK1's three-poses-in-four-steps cadence). A Kintaro beat
MK1-style (he lost as Goro there) was built and then removed.

## How it is wired

`winner_status` gets a fourth value.

* `finish_him_sequence` (MAIN.ASM) asks `soul_escape_due` first: carry set when
  the side whose bar is empty is Shang, is **not** on a human's sticks
  (`p?_state`), and the game is one-player. On a yes it jumps to
  `soul_escape_sequence` instead of running FINISH HIM, and that retp's back
  when Shang is down, so the winner is declared exactly as before.
* `soul_escape_sequence` sets `winner_status = 4`. `check_winner_status`
  (JOY.ASM) dispatches it through `status_jumps` to `soul_escape_status` in
  **both** fighters' processes: the loser runs the whole show from his own
  process, the winner stands and watches until the status goes back to 1 or 2.
* `boss_minumum_diff` (MAIN.ASM) puts the one-player CPU Shang fight on
  `curback 8`, next to the Kintaro/Kahn override that was already there. Stage
  only - his difficulty ramp is untouched.

Three places had to learn about the new value, and each one is a real bug
without it:

| where | why |
|---|---|
| `check_stay_down` (MKREACT.ASM) | it only lets a fallen fighter get up on status 0 or 3; on anything else it compares the status against the process id and keeps him down. Under status 4 **both** men would lie there for ever, and Shang has to be on his feet to reach the handler at all. |
| `strike_check` (JOY.ASM) | the winner is only parked when he next polls, between moves, so a strike already in flight could catch Shang mid-sequence and hand his process to a hit reaction - the show would stop with him in it. While `f_soulesc` is set nothing lands, the same way `f_kahn_phase` phases projectiles through Kahn. |
| `check_winner_status` bound | it errors out (`winner_error`) above 3. |

The souls and their flames are a **fixed pool of four each**, built once and
recycled round-robin: a soul lives ~95 ticks and they come every 27, so four is
always enough. Sixteen create/delete pairs would not be - hand-deleting `gmo`
objects is what wedged Kahn's glow, and the safe unlink leaks a palette
reference every time. `swpal` on a pooled object is a clean swap, so their
palettes never pile up either.

## Kintaro finishes it

MK1 ends here; MK2 does not, because the man who watched still has two fights
left. The arena prop in the stands stops being scenery.

**The prop leaves first.** `arena_goro_leap` (ARENAKIN.ASM) plays
`SMTIGERJUMP1-4` at 2 ticks a frame, rising `arena_goro_leap_dy` = 50 px on
each - 200 px over four frames, which takes him off the top of a 254-line
screen from anywhere in the stands. The `set_inviso` at the end is a backstop;
the rise is what the player watches him leave on. The two Kintaros must never
share a frame, which is the whole reason that number is what it is.

**Then the fight-size one plays the real move, three times.** Not a lane
written for this: `goro_psycho_stomp` (MKBOSS.ASM), the teleport-and-drop, on
a bare object - picked up at the point the player can actually see it.

| beat | where it comes from |
|---|---|
| `087h` and the long roar `0b1h`, and he is gone from the floor | the move opens on those two |
| 8 ticks with nothing on screen | its own `sleep 8` at the top of the climb |
| back down over the body on `TIGERJUMP5`, the pounce, held all the way | `do_next_a9_frame` after `stop_me`; `flight_call` passes `never_ani`, so the move does not animate through the fall either |
| falling at `040000h` under `010000h` | `flight_call`'s own two arguments |
| `TIGERJUMP1` on the floor | `goro_psycho_land_frame` - `do_first_a9_frame` on ani `00ah` |
| `shake_a11 0000a000fh` and `0b2h` | its landing |

**The climb is deliberately not drawn.** The move sets `ograv` NEGATIVE and
drives him under power until `distance_off_ground` passes `0110h` - 272 px,
off the top of a 254-line screen - so on screen it already *is* a
disappearance. It was drawn as a visible arc twice before this: once powered
and capped at 144 px, once ballistic off `goro_stupid_jump`'s
`-0a0000h`/`0c000h` (about 67 px, the ordinary in-fight jump). Both read as
Kintaro leaping absurdly high for a stomp in place, three times in a row. Only
the drop is played.

He arrives that way too - the prop in the stands did the first disappearance
for him - and after the third landing he pounces away (`soul_kin_bail`, which
IS a visible take-off: it is him leaving under his own power, not
teleporting). The object is deleted once he is clear of the frame.

**Physics, on an object that is not a player.** `flight` / `flight_call`
(MOVES.ASM) are no use here - they live in `*a13(p_ganiy)`, `p_store4`,
`get_char_ani`, `init_anirate` and `ground_player`, and this is a bare object
whose a13 belongs to Shang. What they rely on is available though: the display
walker adds `oyvel` to `oyval` for every object on the list (`dclipl`,
MKDISP.ASM), which is why `flight_loop` only sleeps and polls `oypos`. Nothing
adds `ograv` to `oyvel` for a bare one - that is the player path - so the drop
loop does that itself, one add a tick.

**Everything that sleeps is a process routine.** `soul_kin_part`,
`soul_kin_fall`, `soul_kin_land`, `soul_kin_hop` and
`soul_kin_bail` are all `jsrp`/`retp`. A `callr`'s return address lives on the
shared system stack and does not survive the dispatcher - `arena_goro_leap`
itself was written as a plain `callr` once and played one frame, then parked in
the stands for ever. `soul_kin_frame` does not sleep, so it stays a `callr`,
but it hands back everything except a0: the drop loop carries its floor in
a11 across it and a `calla` owes it nothing.

**The winner feels the first one.** Kintaro hitting the floor is Jax's ground
pound as far as he is concerned, so he takes the pound's own reaction -
`do_quake` (MOVES.ASM) hands its victim `r_quake`, and so does this - and comes
out of his victory pose for it. It is raised as a flag rather than a takeover
because the winner is parked in `soul_escape_status`'s idle loop in his own
process, which is exactly where a reaction wants to be entered from; he gets
up, and the loop puts him back on his victory.

`soul_kintaro_knock` has **no range test**. It had one, and at the distance the
two of them actually finish the souls apart - 120-odd px - it never fired.
`do_quake` has none either: the pound is a shockwave through the whole floor,
not a hit box. Only the FIRST landing raises it: `f_soul_knock` is a one-shot
the idle loop clears on its way into `r_quake`, so raising it on all three
would queue a knockdown for whenever he stands up, which is after the stomp is
over.

## The burst, and what is left of him

What the third stomp leaves bursts on **Ermac's** blood explosion, not Shao
Kahn's fist pulp: `soul_stomp_blood` (MKBLOOD.ASM, `blood_procs` slot `01bh`)
is `ermac_splash_blood`'s three `BLDXPD1-13` splats with the step moved out of
the `[Y:X]` high half into the low one and widened from 24 px to 40, because
the body it is bursting is lying down, not standing up. Going through
`create_blood_proc` means the `f_no_blood` DIP and `getprc`'s `MAX_BLOOD` cap
both still apply for free.

**Where it goes is measured.** A fighter face down on the floor still carries
the STANDING origin - about 80 px in the air - so lining the splats up on his
anchor, which is exactly right for Ermac's upright victim, set the burst off
over Kintaro's chest. `soul_kintaro_finish` takes the body's real bottom edge
off `mpart_bounds`, lifts it `soul_burst_lift` and hands the point in as `a9`
(`kahn_knee_blood`'s own contract, slot `016h`); `soul_stomp_blood` adds the
spread to it. a9's low half is zero, so `010000h-ssb_dx` lands as `[Y:-40]`
exactly and borrows nothing out of Y - a bare `movi -40` would set every high
bit and throw that splat a screen upwards, because `adjust_blood_a0` reads x
with `sext a0,w` and y with `sra 16,a1`.

**And he does not simply vanish under it.** `soul_shang_parts` puts three of
his OWN gore records down on the stone where he was - head, torso and legs, a
little apart, and then the body itself goes `set_inviso` under them:

| piece | record | where it is from |
|---|---|---|
| head | `STHEAD1` | the first frame of `a_head`, the decapitated head's roll |
| torso | `STDECAPTORSO7` | `NJDECAP7` - the last frame of his decapitation fall |
| legs | `STDECAPLEG7A` | the other half of the same frame |

No new art, and nothing to pack: that frame is already a body flat on the
floor and already cut at the waist. `change_to_bloody_pal` reads `*a8(ochar)`,
so the ochar is set to `ft_st` first and the three come out in his gore
colours.

They are **single-part** objects (`gso_dmawnz`), so there is no piece list and
no shape RAM to find for them - and, usefully, a single-part object's
`oxpos`/`oypos` ARE its top-left corner, which is also how `mpart_bounds`
reports one. So they are placed outright off two numbers measured from the
body: its bottom edge and its left edge, spaced along the floor from there.
`lineup_1pwm` + `multi_adjust_xy` - `decapped_head`'s own contract - was tried
first and is the wrong tool here: it works off the STANDING anipoint of a body
that is lying down, and left the head hanging in mid air.

`soul_part_lift` is the one fudge. `mpart_bounds` measures the RECORDS, and the
prone frame's strips run about 24 px past his last drawn row, so resting a
piece on the raw bottom puts it through the stone and off the bottom of the
screen. It is the same overshoot `soul_burst_lift` corrects for the explosion.

**z is what stacks it.** The pieces are `front_z`, the burst is `front_z+1` and
Kintaro is `front_z-1`: the remains lie on the floor in front of him and
UNDER the explosion.

## The sky flash

MK1 flashed the dark doorway behind Shang's throne. Kahn's Arena has no hole in
its art to flash through, but its back plane `baklst7` is the fire-glow sky and
nothing else, so `soul_sky_flash` walks that plane, collects the distinct colour
maps on it (up to four) and pushes white into them and back every 4 ticks
through `palset`. A raster band cannot do this - see `project_kahn_sky_shimmer`:
a display register moves every plane on its rows, foreground included.

## What the test proves

`tools/shang_soul_escape_test.lua` plays a real one-player game - coin, start,
Liu Kang, the CPU is Shang - pins P1's bar and holds Shang's at 1 so a clean hit
ends each round. Nothing about the ending is forced. PASS requires all of:

* `curback` is 8 when the fight starts - chosen by the ROM, not the script
* `winner_status` never reads 3, and does read 4
* exactly **16** souls spawn (rising edges of `pid_soul`), peaking at 4 alive
* `f_soulesc` goes 1 → -1 → 0
* the winner is declared afterwards
* **`battle_num` increments** - the next battle really starts. A wedged
  round-end parks at `gstate 5` with the last pose still up and looks exactly
  like a finished sequence to a sampler, so "it played" is not evidence.

Measured: stage 8, status 4 at frame 2622, 16 souls, peak 4, `f_soulesc` 1 →
-1 over 702 ticks, winner declared, `battle_num 1 → 2` at gstate 7.
`-Mode control` (an ordinary CPU opponent) gets FINISH HIM, never status 4, and
also advances. `tools/run_battle_stability_sweep.ps1`: 27/27, 0 issues.
