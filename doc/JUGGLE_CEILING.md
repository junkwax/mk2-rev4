# Juggle damage ceiling

Generated by `tools/juggle_ceiling.py`. **These are upper bounds,
not combos.** Nothing here checks that the listed moves can follow
one another in the air, and several cannot - the number bounds the
balance question rather than answering it. Section 4.3 warns that
finding the real routes by driving inputs measures the rig's
cadence rather than the game, so this does not try.

Health is **161**, not 100. Observed strings run 2.1-3.4 hits with
a longest of 5, so the 3-hit column is the realistic shape and the
5-hit column is the hard cap `p_hit > 3` allows.
