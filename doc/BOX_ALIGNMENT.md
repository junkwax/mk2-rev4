# Strike box vs the frame it is live on

Generated by `tools/box_align_audit.py`.
`striker`/`striker_2` play part one of the animation and THEN hold
its last frame for the strike window, so that frame is what the box
has to match. Positive gap = the box starts that many pixels past
the right edge of what is drawn.

SCOPE: only handlers that dispatch through `striker` or `striker_2`.
A move that scans per tick inside its own flight loop - kahn_fly, and
MK3's sk_air_charge it is ported from - has no single contact pose to
align against, and is deliberately not listed here.

Across 216 resolved moves the box reaches a median of +0 px past
the art - a few pixels of grace on the end of a fist. That is the
scale a finding here has to be judged against.

MK3 IS NOT A DEFENCE AGAINST THIS. UMK3's `striker` (REV21/MKSTK.ASM)
is the same routine instruction for instruction - part one plays, then
its final pose is held for the scan. Anything ported over from MK3
inherits the same risk and has to be checked here too - and a box
ported with a move was sized for THAT GAME'S ART, which is a second
way for the two to disagree: MK3's `stk_sk_hammer` front edge is 086h
(134 px) while our BGHAMMERTOP6 hammer piece only draws to +126, so
8 px of the active box is empty air.

A PINNED RIG CANNOT CONFIRM A ROW FOR A MOVE THAT TRAVELS. Holding
the pair at a fixed distance overwrites oxpos before a lunge, charge
or dive can carry the attacker, so the rig reports the static box
rather than the move - Kahn's air knee measured 72 px that way and is
actually 224. Release the pin at move start, or check the handler
calls init_special -> stop_me and never sets a velocity.

## How this can be wrong - read before believing a row

A handler names one animation index and one strike index, and both
resolve PER CHARACTER. For a move dispatched through an `ochar_*`
table the reachable characters are known, and the row says
`ochar table`. Everything else is applied to all seventeen and says
`assumed` - and a character who cannot reach that handler produces
a row that looks alarming and means nothing. A resolved pose named
`*STANCE*` or `*DIE*` is the giveaway. Trust `ochar table` rows;
confirm `assumed` ones by hand.

### Set aside: 7 rows that are not evidence

Every one is an `assumed` row - the handler is not dispatched
through an `ochar` table, so it was applied to all seventeen
characters - and one of two things then gives it away:

* **pose is idle art** - it resolved onto a stance, walk or
  death frame, which means the character does not have the
  move (Sub-Zero does not have Kung Lao's backfist);
* **boss ani table** - Kintaro and Shao Kahn do not use the
  standard layout at all (24 and 26 entries against 67), so an
  index-based lookup against a normal-cast handler is
  meaningless for them: `ani1[21]` is a sweep for the cast and
  `a_shredded` for the bosses.

Neither says anything about a box. Listed so the count above
is honest.

| `do_wheel_kick` | baraka, noob, reptile, scorpion, smoke, sub-zero | pose is idle art |
