# MK3 NIGHTWOLF - Wolf only, ochar 23, a START tap on Liu Kang

Nightwolf from MK3, on the Wolf build only. **Status (2026-10-12): selectable,
his body and all four specials are in and proven, with his own MK3 sounds
and voice.** Fatalities, his VS portrait and the CPU using his specials are
still to come.

## How to reach him

On the select screen, put the cursor on **Liu Kang** and tap **START** (tap
again to go back). The box wipes from Liu Kang's face into Nightwolf's, as
Mileena's does into Sindel's. Confirm as usual. Always unlocked. He has no
Shang Tsung morph yet (MK3 has one, `do_st_2_ind`).

## TABLE1

MK2 rows 0-29h map onto his MK3 lanes by meaning (`a_zap` = the bow at 26h,
`a_projectile` 27h, `a_throw` his body slam). Four lanes MK2 walks one part
further than MK3 lays them out - block, duck block, knockdown, flip kick - are
rebuilt in MK2's shape from his own frames (`nw_mk2_0b/0c/0f/1e`, the
`MK2_SHAPE` lesson from Sindel). Row 42h (Liu Kang's backfist) is his high
punch.

Rows MK3 has no lane for - **thrown by each MK2 fighter (2Ah-35h) and the
fatality-victim rows (25h, 36h-41h)** - are Liu Kang's MK2 lanes copied in
(`nwt_` / `nwk_`), because their offsets are timed to MK2's own throws and
fatalities. In every copied lane Liu Kang's STUMBLE / FLIPPED / KNOCKDOWN /
STUNNED / SWEEPUP / HITHI / HITLO / SWEEPFALL frames (and his FB / FLIP thrown
frames) are swapped for Nightwolf's of the same family and number, clamped to
his last - 90 frames. **The gore frames are still Liu Kang's art** (decap,
torso rip, impale, back break, head hole, nut crunch), drawn in Nightwolf's
palette, so they come out in the wrong colours: Nightwolf has no gore in MK2's
shape. Open item.

## His specials (`src/NWOLFM.ASM`)

MK3's own inputs and routines, ported call for call; damage is MK3's (the
strike records' damage words) and MK2's combo scaling applies on top.

| move | input | MK3 routine | strike row | measured |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| arrow | Down, Back + LP | `do_ind_zap` / `ind_zap_proc`: `a_zap` part one draws the bow (a slave), the arrow flies at 0a0000h on part three's loop, part four bursts it | 17h (`stk_ind_zap`, Liu Kang's fireball reaction) | 16 |
| hatchet uppercut | Down, Forward + HP | `stat_do_axe_up`: TABLE2 row 2, the hatchet a slave, one hit per swing | 19h (`stk_axe_up`, LK uppercut reaction) | 18 |
| reflector | Back, Back, Back + HK | `do_reflect`: TABLE2 row 3, the glow a slave | - | Reptile's spit turned back: he takes 18 |
| shoulder charge | Forward, Forward + LK | `ind_charge`: TABLE2 row 4, 9 a tick for 20 ticks, MK3's GREEN shadow: `trail_proc` (MKFX.ASM, as Cage's shadow kick) with MK3's own `ind_shadow_p`, his body re-hued green, carried word for word into NWOLFM.ASM | 18h (`stk_ind_charge`, LK roundhouse reaction) | 24 |

- **The reflector is Shao Kahn's clap.** MK3 turned a projectile round when it
  reached a man in `act_reflect`; MK2 has no such hook, but it has
  `kahn_deflect_proj` (MKZAP.ASM), which hands a projectile in flight to the
  deflector's opponent, reverses it and mirrors its art. `nw_reflect_scan`
  runs it every tick of the glow for any projectile of the other man's whose
  art is within `nw_reflect_reach` (70h) of his anchor. MKZAP now exports
  `kahn_deflect_proj` / `kahn_proj_span` under `.if wunit`. It leaves
  `debug4 = 5020h`, which the rig reads.
- **Too late is too late.** Back,Back,Back pressed while the spit is already
  arriving (the rig's `NW_P1AT=24`) gets him hit before the glow is up - the
  stick queue was proved identical to a working press. In time (p1at 0-6) it
  reflects every run.
- **The charge's shadow is MK3's own**: `create_fx 1` is `trail_proc` - full copies of him three ticks
  apart in `ind_shadow_p` (MK3 MKFX.ASM). MK2's `trail_proc` is the same routine; a `.if wunit` branch at
  `trail6` gives ochar 23 that palette. Spawned AFTER the 9-a-tick velocity (it ends itself when he stops,
  so made during the wind-up it would park a copy on his own spot); `stop_me` ends it and the copies are
  erased one by one. The first cut used the flat-pen ghost ring, armed during the wind-up and left running,
  which read in play as him flickering out. Filmed frame by frame (`NW_ONLY=CHARGE NW_NOWALK=1
  NW_SNAPALL=CHARGE`): he is drawn every frame and `dmaq_late` / `dmaq_lost` do not move.
- His **"open"** handler rows (`char_hip/lop/hik/lok_open`) carry the same four
  handlers, as MK3's `ind_hp_open` did.
- **His sounds are MK3's own** (2026-10-14), carried out of `umk3.zip` by
  `tools/make_wolf_audio.py` (`nwolf_rows`), appended after every earlier row
  (triple_sndtab2 98h-B2h, tracks $068D-$06DD, ~141 KB of streams; u9 still
  has ~296 KB free). All fifteen rows of MK3's `st_indian`, sent where MK3
  sends them: arrow drawn (0) / loosed (1) / hit or blocked (3/4, off `b2`),
  axe up (5) and, on a clean uppercut, axe hit (9 - MK3 plays it from
  `r_axe_up`), reflector (7), shoulder charge (8). Rows 2, 6 and 0Ah-0Eh are
  the fatalities' and are carried unwired. **His voice is MK3's gp_male2, Dan
  Forden**: `rsnd_ochar` (MKSOUND.ASM) sends ochar 23 to `rsnd_mk3df`, which
  swaps MK2's voice KIND for the MK3 row the way `rsnd_mk3fem` does for
  Sonya/Sindel (attack, hit, grab, throw, wasted, tripped, death; MK3's jump
  and "shook" have no MK2 caller). Shao Kahn says "Night Wolf" when he is
  picked (`php_nwolf`, MKSEL, B1h) and "Nightwolf wins" (`winner_speech` row
  23 = B2h). **Both are sent BY NUMBER, and `winner_speech`'s row is
  GENERATED** by `tools/wolf_fighter_rows.py` (`NIGHTWOLF`) - edit it there or
  Phase B2d9 puts the old number back; `make_wolf_audio.py --check` fails if
  either number stops matching the overlay. The axe fatality's thunder is
  still MK2's BOOM (`nw_thunder`).

## Two traps this port hit

- **A 40 KB lane module in the middle of the link** pushed DIAGMENU's and
  DIAGDTST's `callr`s out of range (GSPLNK reports it as a PC-relative
  displacement overflow). `NWOLFL.OBJ` / `NWOLFM.OBJ` are LAST in `mk2.cmd`,
  as `MK1KNL` is, for the same reason.
- **Inserting a routine after a `.if` block's last line ate its `.endif`**
  (`nwolf_select_cheat_apply`, MAIN.ASM) and broke another session's T-unit
  build. Check `.if`/`.endif` balance after every insertion into shared files.

## Verified

`tools/wolf_nightwolf_check.lua` (`tools/run_wolf_boot_probe.ps1 -Script
wolf_nightwolf_check.lua -Env "WS_MAP=<repo>\tmp\wolf\src\mk2.map"`), Wolf ROM,
2-player: START on Liu Kang's square on/off/on reads flag/frame **1/8, 0/0,
1/8**; P1 fights as **ochar 23**; then the reflector (Reptile spits, 18 back on
him, `debug4 5020`), arrow 16, uppercut 18 (walked in), charge 24, no reboot -
**PASS**. `NW_NOTAP=1` is the control (fights as Liu Kang) - **PASS**.
`NW_SND=<abs path to tmp\wolf\src\WOLFSND.ASM>` adds the sound half: it taps
the sound latch and requires each trial's MK3 sounds, Kahn's name call at the
select screen, and a plain HIGH KICK voicing Dan Forden's attack - **PASS**
(the "Nightwolf wins" line is not exercised: the rig pins the bars). Stock
MAME cannot PLAY u6-u9; this proves what the ROM sends.
`NW_TRACE=1` logs both fighters' actions, every HK dispatch and P1's stick
queue. T-unit `mk2.out` byte-identical but for the COFF timestamp.
