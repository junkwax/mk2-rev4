# The move matrix

Generated by `tools/move_matrix.py`. Every number is read out of
MKSTK.ASM, a move handler, or a sprite table - nothing is estimated.

**band** is the PREDICTED connect range against a standing victim:
`x_offset - x_size - W/8 .. x_offset + W/8`, from `strike_check`
and `sq_punch`. It is a prediction, and the two places it is known
to be wrong are named in the tool's own header - travelling moves,
and the near edge at point blank where `repell` intervenes.

Projectiles are absent on purpose: their box rides the projectile
object, so their range is travel over time, not a number here.
