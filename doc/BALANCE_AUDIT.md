# Strike-table outliers

Generated by `tools/balance_audit.py` from `doc/frame_data_records.csv`.
Families are the same move across characters; a member well off the
family median is either a deliberate trait or a mistake.

**This is a screening tool.** It finds things worth looking at, not
things that are wrong. Boss rows are marked - Kintaro and Shao Kahn
being outliers on damage is correct, not a finding.
