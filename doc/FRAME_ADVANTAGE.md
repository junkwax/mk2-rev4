# Startup, active and recovery

Generated by `tools/frame_advantage.py`. Ticks, not milliseconds -
one tick is one display refresh at 54.7068 Hz, **18.28 ms**.

`~` means the handler BRANCHES between the strike loop and the
retract, so its tail is conditional: a whiff skips that sleep and
a CONNECT pays it.

**A blocked strike is a connect.** `striker` returns the carry it
got from strike_check, and strike_blocked ends `jruc yes_collision`,
which sets it exactly as a clean hit does - so a `jrnc` tail is paid
on block too, not on hit only. Measured end to end, the low kick
runs 32 ticks on a whiff and 44 on EITHER a block or a hit
(`tools/run_kick_recovery_test.ps1`). This file used to drop that
sleep from every on-block number, which read the whole kick family
about 18 ticks better on block than it is.

A handler CAN tell the two apart itself, by testing `b2` - the
blocked flag strike_check documents - and skipping its hangtime
on a clean hit; `hit_only` below detects that shape. **No
shipping handler does it.** do_lo_kick did for one build and it
was reverted: the arithmetic worked - -18 became +0 on hit - and
it played wrong, because the leg snapped back the instant it
connected. The hangtime is the impact.

**advantage = stun - recovery**, with no `active - 1` term. The
strike loop EXITS on contact (`jrc attack_hit`), so the rest of the
active window is skipped rather than owed - the low kick ends
in 44 ticks on a connect against 32 on a whiff. Charging the
attacker for ticks he never spends read every move with a long
active window worse than it is.

Blockstun comes from `block_xfers` indexed by the LOW byte of the
strike-routine word, and each reaction's `block_shake` is
2 x a10 x a11 - a four-value ladder of 4, 8, 12 and 16 ticks chosen
per REACTION, not per move.

**On-HIT advantage is still missing.** Hitstun is an `animate_a9`
reaction lane whose row count lives in the VICTIM's anitab, so the
same move has a different advantage depending on who you hit. That
is the remaining half of section 1.3.

Validated against a live tick-by-tick capture of Baraka's Back+HP:
measured 9 startup / 4 active / 14 recovery; extracted **9 / 4 / 14**.

### The 'no handler' bucket is two different problems

A handler is not the only thing that picks a strike record.
Projectiles and several specials store `p_stk` **inline**, deep in
a routine and often more than once — MKPROP's dive does
`movi 014h,a0 / move a0,*a13(p_stk)` eleven lines in, after two
`mframew` calls — so the one-index-per-handler model these tools
use cannot claim them without guessing, and guessing here is how
Baraka's sweep once got credited to `do_fn1_spinchop`.

| | rows | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **contested** | 2 | several handlers claim the index and none is confirmed, so the picker refuses to guess |
| a routine of THIS character sets the index | 1 | attributable, but not by the handler model |
| a routine sets it, owner unresolvable | 58 | the setter sits under an inner label like `dive3`, or is shared |
| **nothing anywhere sets it** | 77 | the record is reached some other way, or is dead |

### The standing punches are measured but deliberately blank

`joy_hi_punch` / `joy_lo_punch` are a sixth dispatch shape: no
`striker` call at all, but `act_mframew` then one `strike_check_a0`
then `punch_sleep`, which runs a strike check on every one of its
`a10` ticks. Parsing it is easy and it would add 24 rows — the
most-used moves in the game — so it was built, and then it was
measured, and the measurement threw it out.

`tools/sweep_addon_jab.lua`, stick forced neutral, Liu Kang's
standing HP: **18 ticks in `act_hipunch`, n=5, zero variance.**
The parse predicts 9 startup + 6 active = 15. And 18 is not
`rows x 3 + 5` for any whole number of rows, so one of three
assumptions is wrong — the part-one row count, `punch_sleep`'s
cost, or the belief that `act_hipunch` covers startup and active
and nothing else (the retract may run under the same action).

Two traps worth keeping, both of which faked a clean answer first:
the sweep walks P1 all fight to stress the camera, so **back+HP is
Liu Kang's backfist** (`act_lkbackfist`, 010ch) and most taps were
measuring that; and at point-blank `elbow_check` turns HP into an
elbow (`act_elbow`, 0114h). The 6-tick runs that looked exactly
like a clean `active` of 1+5 were the backfist's tail.

The parser is kept in `frame_data.parse_mframew_handlers` and its
output is **not merged**. A missing row costs a player nothing; a
wrong one costs them the match.
