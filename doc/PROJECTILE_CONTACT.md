# Projectile contact height

Generated by `tools/projectile_contact_audit.py`.

`match_me_with_him` snaps the burst onto the victim's anchor and
discards the height the projectile was flying at. On a level shot
that changes nothing - the projectile is spawned on the thrower's
anipoint, so its Y is already the victim's anchor Y. It only
matters for a STEERABLE shot, or when the two anchors sit at
different heights.

A discarded height is not automatically a defect. The signals
column is what decides it: a y offset applied after the snap or a
velocity written into the burst means the site is placing the
effect deliberately, and restoring the flight height would break
that rather than fix it.
