Mortal Kombat II · v4.0 · FBNeo / Fightcade set mk2rev4

Move List & Strategy

Every special, fatality, babality and stage fatality in v4.0, checked against the build itself. Moves marked NEW are not in arcade MK2 (L3.1).

How to read it

Inputs
→ toward him← away↑↓ up / down
HP high punchLP low punchHK high kickLK low kickBL block

Arrows assume you face right. Press directions in order, then the button. "Hold … release" means keep the button down, do the rest, then let go.

Fatality range

Close touching, a step at most · Sweep just outside close · Mid a third to half a screen · Far two-thirds of a screen or more.

Finishers work only during FINISH HIM. Babalities need a round with no punches (HP or LP) at all.

What's different in v4.0

  • Hidden fighters: on the select screen, tap START on Scorpion for Ermac, Kitana for Jade, Sub-Zero for Rain. Tap again to switch back. In the FBNeo / Fightcade set all three are open from power-on. On an arcade board, beat Jade in her secret fight, Shao Kahn, or Smoke to unlock Jade, Ermac or Rain.
  • Back-block is on by default: holding away from him blocks.
  • Specials out of block: holding BL no longer stops a special.
  • Perfect block: tap BL just before a hit lands. You take a quarter of the chip and recover first. Holding block does not count.
  • Combo counter and damage scaling: later hits in a string do less. Throws and holds scale too.
  • Anti-juggle: more than three hits in, the attacker is pushed back and held until the victim lands.
  • Draw, then Sudden Death: the first tie is a draw; the next is Sudden Death on 10% health with no chip damage.
  • Costumes: hold START and press HP, LP, HK, LK, BL or insert a coin as you pick. Two players never wear the same colours.
  • No Friendships. Stage fatalities work on The Kombat Tomb, The Pit II, Yoteigai Tor and The Pit (MK1). On The Dead Pool, an uppercut during FINISH HIM throws him into the acid.

Secret opponents

Jade

One-player: on the match right before the "?" on the Battle Plan, win a round using only LK. No HP, LP, HK or Block at all. You go straight to a fight with Jade.

Smoke

On The Portal, when Dan Forden pops up with "Toasty!" after an uppercut, quickly press ↓+START.

Noob Saibot

One-player: on the Battle Plan screen between matches, hold START about 3 seconds without letting go.

Khameleon

One-player: on the Battle Plan screen, tap START five times.

Strategy notes

  • Lead with your best hit. Scaling makes later hits worth less.
  • Standing Back + HP is a new poke for most of the cast. Crouching Down + HP is still the uppercut.
  • Steer your projectiles. Most of them take Up / Down in flight, so jumping them is harder.
  • Tap block, don't hold it when you expect a hit: perfect blocks let you punish.
  • Scorpion's teleports are a mix-up: Down, Back lands behind; Down, Forward lands in front. They share one cooldown.
  • The bosses fight back. Kintaro catches jump-ins out of the air, and both bosses punish what they block.
